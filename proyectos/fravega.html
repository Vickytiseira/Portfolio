04  Case study

Frávega Seller Center

Simplifying daily operations for marketplace sellers managing large catalogs.

Product
Seller Center, the back office for third-party sellers on Frávega's marketplace
Industry
E-commerce, marketplace
My role
Senior UX/UI Designer
Team
6-person design squad: UX writers, UX researchers and UI/UX designers
Timeline
2020 to 2022

01  The problem

Third-party sellers manage catalogs of 1,000+ products, invoices, account statements and their own team from one back office.

Every task touches money or stock, so unclear states and generic errors turn into support tickets and lost sales.

Large catalogs1,000+ SKUs per seller.
Different rolesAdministrators, price and stock editors, viewers.
Money and invoicingErrors have real financial cost.
A shared design systemComponents had to serve the whole company.

02  Approach

Inside the squad's Discovery and Design Thinking process, we ran moderated usability tests with at least 5 users per round, user interviews and Maze surveys, and used SQL, analytics and heatmaps to find friction and measure each feature's impact.

I also designed Frávega Credits, the financing flow in the marketplace checkout, as part of the same work.

03  Key decisions

Four decisions made operations easier to read and harder to get wrong.

1

Publishing status always visible

The catalog shows each product's state (Incomplete, Approved, Pending approval) with search and filters by category and brand.

TradeoffA busier list, in exchange for sellers knowing at a glance what needs their attention.

2

One form for one product, bulk upload for many

The new product form validates required fields inline, and bulk upload covers large catalogs.

TradeoffTwo ways to do the same task to maintain, because a seller adding one product and one migrating hundreds have different needs.

3

Roles that match how teams work

Users and roles (Administrator, Price editor, Stock editor, Viewer, among others) let each seller decide who can change what.

TradeoffMore setup for the seller, in exchange for fewer mistakes on prices and stock.

4

Errors that say what to fix

Invoices show specific error messages per order, and destructive actions ask for explicit confirmation.

TradeoffMore messages to write and maintain, instead of one generic error that sends sellers to support.

04  Collaboration

I worked within a 6-person design squad with UX writers and researchers, and with product and engineering in each feature's discovery and delivery.

The components designed for Seller Center and Credits fed Frávega's Design System, now used across the company's products, from back-office tools to customer-facing platforms.

05  Impact

Seller Center components became part of Frávega's Design System, used across the company's products.
