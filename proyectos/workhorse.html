03  Case study

Workhorse

Bringing order to an electric truck and fleet management platform, as the only designer.

Product
Customizable electric truck platform and fleet management system
Industry
Electric vehicles, logistics
My role
Product Designer, sole designer (via Southteams)
Team
Product Manager, front-end and back-end developers, engineers
Timeline
2022 to 2024, 2 years

01  The problem

The platform had grown without structure: fragmented flows, inconsistent interfaces and a steep learning curve.

Fleet operators, technicians and administrators struggled to configure vehicles, read real-time data and manage alerts. They couldn't tell where a truck was, which route it was on, or who was responsible for a repair.

Limited access to usersResearch had to rely on other sources.
Technical and non-technical usersManagers and field technicians, one platform.
Dense real-time dataStatus, location, alerts and history.
One designerEvery flow, plus the design system.

02  Approach

With limited access to end users, I combined heuristic audits, benchmarking of B2B fleet and EV platforms, and interviews with product managers, engineers and sales leads, plus insights from support.

The audit pointed to three problems: no real-time feedback during setup, inconsistent components across modules, and users feeling stuck in configuration flows without a way to go back or save progress. Two proto-personas, a fleet manager and an EV technician, kept the work grounded.

03  Key decisions

Four decisions organized the platform around what each user needed to see first.

1

A dashboard built for scanning

A structured table with status indicators, sorting and filters, plus a clear navigation sidebar.

TradeoffA denser, less visual view than cards, chosen because fleet managers need to compare many vehicles and cases at a glance.

2

Location and health, side by side

A real-time map shows cases and vehicle status by location, next to a case list.

TradeoffThe map shares the screen with the list instead of taking it over, so users keep the detail while seeing the geography.

3

One record per vehicle

A single vehicle record brings together specs, configuration and service history.

TradeoffA longer page in exchange for one source of truth that technicians and managers share.

4

Complex forms, grouped by topic

Creating a case or a company groups related information into labeled sections, with a troubleshooting guide where it helps.

TradeoffMore visible structure on each form, in exchange for fewer errors and a clear step-by-step path.

04  Collaboration

I delivered end-to-end flows, high-fidelity responsive screens, prototypes, specs and handoff documentation, working with engineering across agile sprints. I also ran sprint planning.

The reusable design system was documented in Storybook and ReactCosmos, so new modules started from shared components.

05  Impact

Nine core flows and modules redesigned across the platform.
