05  Case study

Market Eye

Turning photos of retail refrigerators into commercial decisions.

Product
Retail intelligence platform for AB InBev's commercial teams
Industry
Consumer goods, retail
My role
UX/UI Designer
Client
AB InBev
Year
2021

01  The problem

Cameras inside refrigerators at points of sale capture an image every 30 minutes, and AI identifies availability, out-of-stock items, competitors, shelf share and shelf conditions.

Users needed to understand the whole network, find opportunities and see what was actually happening inside one refrigerator, without being overwhelmed by data.

Many data sourcesMarket, store and image data.
AI detectionsOutputs need context to be trusted.
Different levels of analysisFrom the network to one fridge.
System capabilitiesThe model balanced user needs and what the data could support.

02  Approach

Discovery showed that data needed hierarchy, that AI outputs needed context, and that analysis happens at different levels.

The interaction model was shaped with stakeholders and engineering, balancing user needs, available data and system capabilities.

03  Key decisions

Three decisions connected market-level questions to the evidence inside a single refrigerator.

1

Three levels of analysis

A path from “How are we performing?” to “Where is the opportunity?” to “What is actually happening inside this refrigerator?”.

TradeoffMore screens to navigate than one dense dashboard, so each level shows only what that question needs.

2

Detections drawn on the image

AI detections appear directly on the refrigerator photo, paired with a summary of detected products and their status.

TradeoffA heavier screen than a table of results, in exchange for immediate context: users see where each detection happened.

3

The same filters and colors everywhere

A collapsible navigation and one filter structure across dashboards (level, region, date and time), and competitors keep the same color in every chart.

TradeoffLess freedom per chart, so users never have to relearn a visualization.

04  Collaboration

I designed the web experience with stakeholders and engineering, aligning each view with the data the system could reliably provide.

05  Impact

The platform launched and became the tool AB InBev's commercial teams use to follow market share and check each point of sale.
